This folder should contain 3 audio clips for the Smart Relaxation Room:

1. calm_audio.wav (or .mp3)
   - Soft nature sounds, rain, or ambient pad
   - Suggested search: "nature ambient loop" on freesound.org

2. energy_audio.wav (or .mp3)
   - Upbeat lo-fi, light percussion, or cheerful ambient
   - Suggested search: "upbeat ambient loop" on freesound.org

3. focus_audio.wav (or .mp3)
   - Minimal, steady tone or soft lo-fi beats
   - Suggested search: "focus study ambient" on freesound.org

All clips should be short loops (30–120 seconds) for best results.
Drag each clip into the EnvironmentManager Inspector in Unity.
