## Scenario / Application Domain

The project is set within the domain of **interactive wellness environments** and **adaptive digital media systems**. It is specifically targeted at university students who regularly experience emotional states of stress, fatigue, or reduced focus — conditions that are common in high-pressure academic settings.

The environment is a virtual 3D room built in Unity that detects and responds to the user's current emotional state. It does not rely on biometric sensors or complex machine learning models; instead, it uses a **rule-based context-aware system** that responds to explicit mood input and implicit spatial behaviour (player movement) to alter the environment in real time.

---

## Target User

**University students** who need a calm, focused, or energised digital space during study breaks, before examinations, or at any point when they feel overwhelmed or unfocused.

---

## Intelligent Behaviour

The system demonstrates intelligent behaviour through two complementary mechanisms:

1. **Explicit mood selection** — The user clicks a button (Stressed, Tired, or Unfocused) to declare their current emotional state. The system interprets this and activates the appropriate environmental mode.

2. **Implicit proximity detection** — Without any UI interaction, the system monitors the player's position within the room. If the player walks into the Relax Zone, the environment automatically activates Calm Mode. Walking into the Focus Zone activates Focus Mode. Approaching the lamp or plant triggers localised sensory feedback.

This dual-input approach creates a system that feels responsive and context-aware without requiring complex AI, which aligns with the concept of a **rule-based intelligent agent**.

---

## Planned Adaptive Features

| Trigger | System Response |
|---|---|
| Stressed button / Relax Zone | Soft blue lighting, calm audio, text: "Take a slow breath" |
| Tired button / Energy Zone | Warm orange lighting, energetic audio, text: "Refresh your mind" |
| Unfocused button / Focus Zone | Bright white lighting, focus audio, text: "Stay focused" |
| Player near Lamp | Lamp light activates, soft atmosphere message |
| Player near Plant | Encouraging wellness message displayed |
| Reduce Effects button | Lower audio and light intensity — accessibility feature |

---

## Intended User Experience

The user should feel immediately welcomed and guided. Instructions are always visible on screen. The mood selection area is simple and clear. The environment changes smoothly and meaningfully in response to every interaction. The Reduce Effects feature ensures users with sensory sensitivity are also supported, reflecting a **human-centred design** philosophy.

The overall experience is designed to feel calming, non-overwhelming, and easy to navigate — even for users who have never used a 3D interactive environment before.

## Self-Study Innovation

The project integrates an **emotion-aware adaptive environment** as its self-study innovation feature. This concept draws from research in affective computing and human-computer interaction — specifically the idea that digital environments can adapt their sensory properties based on a user's emotional context. The implementation demonstrates independent learning beyond the curriculum by applying these concepts in a practical, interactive system.
